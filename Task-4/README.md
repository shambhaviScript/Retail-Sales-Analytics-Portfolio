# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis Project
## Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to identify business trends, profitability patterns, and factors affecting business performance.

The project combines:
- Data Storytelling
- Statistical Validation
- Business Insights
- Data Visualization

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance
- Identify key factors affecting profitability
- Understand customer and regional trends
- Validate findings statistically

---

# 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains retail transactional data including:
- Sales
- Profit
- Discount
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Product Category

---

# 📊 Analysis Performed

## 🔹 Regional Analysis
- West region had highest sales
- Central region showed lower profit

## 🔹 Category Analysis
- Technology category most profitable
- Furniture had high sales but low profit

## 🔹 Discount Analysis
- Discounts above 20% reduced profitability
- Strong negative relationship between discount and profit

## 🔹 Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer segment generated highest sales
- Corporate segment showed stable profit

---

# 📈 Statistical Validation

## Hypothesis Testing

### Null Hypothesis (H₀)
Discount has no significant impact on profit.

### Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)
Discount significantly impacts profit.

### Result
- Negative correlation observed
- p-value < 0.05

### Conclusion
Discount strategy significantly affects profitability.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Reduce excessive discounting
- Improve pricing strategy
- Focus on profitable categories
- Strengthen low-profit regions
- Monitor both sales and profit together
